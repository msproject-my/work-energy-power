Potential Energy

•

Potential energy, Ep is the energy possessed by an object due to its position.

•

Gravitational potential energy is part of potential energy.

•

The potential energy depends on the mass and height from ground level.

•

Potential energy also referred to as work done against gravity.

•

The unit for potential energy is Nm @ Joule ( J )

Ep = mgh
m = mass ( kg )
g = gravity ( 9.81 ms-2 )
h = height from the ground ( m )

Kinetic Energy

•

Kinetic energy, Ek is the energy an object possesses due to its motion.

•

The kinetic energy of a moving object depends on its mass and velocity.

•

The unit for kinetic energy is Nm @ Joule ( J )

•

The greater the value of mass or velocity, the higher the kinetic energy possessed by an object.

Ek = ½ mv²
m = Mass ( kg )
v = velocity ( ms-1 )

Principle of Conservation of Energy

The principle of conservation of energy states that energy can neither be created nor destroyed, but can be converted from one form to another. In an isolated system, the total mechanical energy remains 100% constant throughout all elevations.

Assume maximum energy is 100%, therefore:

Ep + Ek = 100% (Constant)
Total Mechanical Energy = Constant
